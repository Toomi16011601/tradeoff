Good adoption evidence is successful use in independent projects. Stars are a secondary signal; no design can guarantee them. Avoid marketed “leak-free,” global robustness, profit scores, first-ever claims or unsupported execution backend claims. Losing public baselines and causal-variant scope must stay visible. [Acceptance evidence](verification.md) records local checks; a checklist entry does not replace those results.
